## Last year

DevFest İstanbul 2025 took place on 7 December 2025 at UNIQ İstanbul with **44 speakers, 39 sessions,
4 tracks and more than 2,500 attendees**. Some rooms were named after Istanbul landmarks: Hagia Sophia,
Galata Tower and Maiden's Tower. Sessions included Jason Mayes on web AI agents and Abdel Sghiouar's
*"Yes you can run LLMs on GKE!"*. The [archive](https://devfest.istanbul/archive) has the full schedules for 2024 and 2025.

## About the look

This site and the demo borrow the visual language of the DevFest İstanbul 2026 poster: curly braces,
outlined pills, a pixel-art Bosphorus and Google's four colors. The pixel skyline (Galata Tower, the Bosphorus Bridge,
a city ferry, Kız Kulesi and a mosque) is drawn from scratch by `scripts/make_skyline.py`.
